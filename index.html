Frequently Asked

Questions we get every week.

Is Trillion Trader 369 a signal-selling service?+

No. Our Telegram community is free to join. We earn through prop firm and broker referral partnerships and optional mentorship, never through selling raw signals.

Do you guarantee profits?+

No — trading carries real risk of loss and no result is guaranteed. We teach process and risk management; see our full disclaimer below.

Which prop firms and brokers do you recommend?+

Only ones our own team actively trades with and has personally withdrawn payouts from. See the Prop Firms and Brokers sections above.

Are the trading tools free to use?+

Yes, all four calculators on this site — Lot Size, Risk Management, P&L, and Pip Value — are free, with no signup required.

How do I get mentorship or premium support?+

Reach out through the Contact section below or message us on Telegram/WhatsApp, and our team will walk you through available options.
